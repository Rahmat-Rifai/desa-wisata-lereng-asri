## Brand & Style
This design system embodies a modern eco-tourism experience that harmonizes the pristine serenity of rural highlands with inviting Indonesian hospitality. Built mobile-first for travelers, weekend wanderers, and agritourism enthusiasts navigating on-the-go, the aesthetic merges clean editorial minimalism with earthy tactile warmth.

Key emotional pillars:
- **Asri & Segar (Fresh & Lush):** Visual rhythm evocative of misted mountain slopes, terraced rice paddies, and crisp hillside air.
- **Ramah & Hangat (Warm & Welcoming):** Tactile, grounding nuances that invite community connection and village authenticity.
- **Jernih & Terpercaya (Clear & Dependable):** Uncluttered layouts, high accessibility, and instant booking paths prioritizing conversion and effortless legibility.

## Colors
The palette balances verdant organic tones against warm mineral accents and breathable alpine whites:

- **Primary (`#1B4D3E` - Forest Emerald):** The foundational anchor representing dense pine slopes and lush terraced fields. Used for primary navigation, deep headings, key active surfaces, and primary buttons.
- **Secondary (`#C86D44` - Terracotta Clay):** An earthy sun-baked ceramic tone providing warmth, contrast, and appetite-inducing vitality. Used for badges, price tags, cultural highlights, and secondary callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#25D366` - WhatsApp Vibrant Green):** Dedicated solely to high-intent conversion actions, instant chat triggers, and floating reservation anchors to ensure maximum clarity and instant recognition.
- **Neutral (`#24332C` - Deep Peat Black):** A soft, bio-tinted charcoal that avoids the harshness of pure black while guaranteeing high-contrast readability against pale surfaces.
- **Canvas & Surface Palette:** Surfaces draw from morning highland mist (`#F8FAF8`), crisp white container fills (`#FFFFFF`), and soft moss-tinted borders (`#E2EBE5`).

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the single typographic voice across all roles. Its geometric precision and humanist warmth mirror contemporary Indonesian design excellence while offering supreme mobile legibility outdoors in direct sunlight.

- **Headlines:** Set tightly tracked with bold weights to create confidence and clear section boundaries.
- **Body:** Generous 1.5–1.6 line heights preserve open, readable rhythm across bilingual or lengthy village tour itineraries.
- **Labels & Badges:** Distinct uppercase or semi-bold micro-copy guarantees effortless scanning of tour amenities, elevation metrics, and seasonal weather tags.

## Layout & Spacing
A disciplined 4px/8px modular base rhythm supports a mobile-first fluid grid.

- **Mobile Viewport (&lt;640px):** Single-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) outer canvas margins and 16px gutter spacing. Horizontal card carousels edge-bleed into margins with subtle peek triggers.
- **Tablet (640px–1023px):** Fluid 6-column grid with 32px (`2rem`) margins. Tour packages and homestay units reflow into clean 2-column cards.
- **Desktop (&ge;1024px):** Fixed-max content constraint (1200px) centered with 12-column grid, 24px gutters, and 48px margins. Sticky booking sidebars remain docked beside narrative itinerary flows.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth mimics natural diffuse highland sunlight—soft, gentle, and free of murky black shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base page background set to `#F8FAF8`. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** White background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a subtle 1px border (`#E2EBE5`) with an ambient diffused drop: `0px 4px 16px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Navigation):** Header bars and interactive filters: `0px 8px 24px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.08)` paired with frosted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over 92% alpha fill).
- **Level 3 (Action Anchors & WhatsApp Float):** The floating WhatsApp quick-inquiry button uses a radiant, warm-tinted elevation: `0px 10px 28px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.35)` to ensure persistent visual dominance above all scrolling village media.

## Shapes
A roundedness level of 2 (0.5rem standard radius) gives the UI a welcoming, approachable, and organic contour without feeling childlike. 

- **Micro elements (Badges, Tags, Form Fields):** 0.5rem (`8px`) border radius.
- **Containers (Cards, Modals, Itinerary Accordions):** `rounded-lg` at 1rem (`16px`).
- **Interactive Action Buttons & Floating WhatsApp:** Full pill contour (`9999px`) to emphasize touch comfort and prompt immediate tap action.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book/Explore):** Forest Emerald background (`#1B4D3E`), crisp white text, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0.75rem 1.75rem`, subtle press scale transition (0.98).
- **WhatsApp Action (Direct Booking & Quick Chat):** Dedicated WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`) fill with pure white label and official WhatsApp icon. Available as a full-width bottom sheet button on mobile or a floating action anchor (FAB) positioned bottom-right (`margin: 1.25rem`).
- **Secondary / Terakota Button:** Outline or solid Terracotta (`#C86D44`) for package upgrades, local culinary guides, and cultural experiences.

### Cards
- **Tour & Homestay Cards:** Pure white surface, 16px border-radius, clean 1px border (`#E2EBE5`), and ambient depth. Top half reserved for 16:10 or 4:3 high-definition landscape photography, paired with an absolute-positioned top badge (e.g., "Pemandangan Gunung", "Family Friendly"). Body holds title, rating indicator, pricing in Terracotta, and full-width instant inquiry button.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Filters:** 8px rounded corners or pills. Unselected: transparent background, 1px border (`#D1DED6`), Forest Emerald text. Selected: Forest Emerald background with white text.
- **Status Tags (Open Season, Weather, Quota):** Soft mint tint (`#E8F4EE`) with Forest Emerald typography, or warm sand tint (`#FBEFE9`) for limited availability.

### Form Inputs & Date Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`.
- Border: 1.5px solid `#D1DED6`, transitioning to `#1B4D3E` on focus with 3px focus ring `rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.15)`.
- Internal spacing: `0.875rem 1rem` for effortless thumb tapping.

### Lists & Itinerary Steps
- Vertical line connector styled in soft herbal green (`#D1DED6`). Step nodes use circular forest badges with clean numerical labels or rural milestone icons (sunrise trek, coffee tasting, river tubing).

### Village Weather & Altitude Banner
- Specialized contextual component at top of screen featuring real-time mountain temperature, mist condition, and altitude badge (e.g., "1.200 mdpl • Sejuk 19°C") set in subtle translucent green glaze.
